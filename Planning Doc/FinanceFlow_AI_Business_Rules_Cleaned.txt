FinanceFlow AI - Business Rules
I recommend the following rules for FinanceFlow AI.
1. Can one payment be allocated to multiple installments?
✅ Yes.
This is important in real-world payments.
Suppose:
Payment received =
 ₹1,50,000
The system finds:
August installment =
 ₹1,00,000
September installment =
 ₹50,000
We can allocate:
₹1,00,000 → August
₹50,000   → September
Our existing design supports this:
PAYMENT
   │
   ├── PAYMENT ALLOCATION → August
   │
   └── PAYMENT ALLOCATION → September
Another example
Expected:
August =
 ₹1,00,000
Received:
Payment 1 =
 ₹40,000
Payment 2 =
 ₹60,000
Both payments can be allocated to the same installment.
August installment
       ↑
       │
   ┌───┴────┐
   │        │
₹40,000   
                  
₹60,000
Business rule
A payment may be split across multiple repayment schedules, and a repayment schedule may receive multiple payments.
This is one of the strongest reasons we need payment_allocations.
2. What happens with overpayment?
Suppose:
Expected =
 ₹1,00,000
Received =
 ₹1,20,000
There is:
₹20,000 OVERPAYMENT
We should not automatically assign the extra ₹20,000 somewhere.
Instead:
Payment
₹1,20,000
    ↓
₹1,00,000 → Current installment
₹20,000   → Excess amount
The system should mark:
allocation_type =
 overpayment
and create an alert:
"Payment exceeds the scheduled amount by ₹20,000."
Then the accountant decides what to do.
Possible actions:
1. Apply excess to next installment
2. Keep as customer credit
3. Refund
4. Investigate
For our MVP, I recommend:
Accountant approval required for overpayments.
AI can recommend, but shouldn't automatically decide where excess money goes.
3. What happens when AI confidence is low?
This is very important for our Agentic AI architecture.
Suppose AI receives:
Payment =
 ₹80,000
Possible companies:
ABC Technologies → 94%
XYZ Technologies → 52%
ABC Trading      → 47%
That's dangerous.
We should establish thresholds.
Example:
Confidence >=
 90%
        ↓
High confidence
        ↓
Recommend to accountant
70% - 89%
        ↓
Medium confidence
        ↓
Accountant must review carefully
< 70%
        ↓
Low confidence
        ↓
DO NOT automatically recommend a match
        ↓
Manual investigation
For our project:
AI confidence
Action
≥ 90%
Strong recommendation
70–89%
Review required
< 70%
Manual investigation
Important:
Confidence should never mean "AI is authorized to approve."
It only represents how strongly the AI believes the candidate matches.
4. What if two companies have similar names?
This will definitely happen.
For example:
ABC Technologies Pvt Ltd
ABC Technology Solutions Pvt Ltd
The AI should not rely only on company name.
It should compare multiple signals.
Matching signals
Payment
 │
 ├── Sender name
 ├── Bank account
 ├── Transaction reference
 ├── Payment amount
 ├── Historical payment pattern
 ├── Company details
 └── Active repayment schedules
Example:
Sender:
ABC Technologies Pvt Ltd
Bank Account:
XXXX1234
Database:
ABC Technologies
Bank Account: XXXX1234
        ↓
Strong match
Even if another company has a similar name, the bank account provides a stronger signal.
Our AI agent can return:
Candidate 1
ABC Technologies
Confidence: 94%
Candidate 2
ABC Technology Solutions
Confidence: 41%
Then the accountant sees the candidates.
Rule:
AI must use multiple matching attributes, not company name alone.
5. Who can approve an AI recommendation?
We should use our RBAC system.
I recommend:
Admin
   ✅ Approve
   ✅ Reject
   ✅ Override
Manager
   ✅ Approve
   ✅ Reject
   ✅ Override
Accountant
   ✅ Review
   ✅ Approve
   ✅ Reject
   ⚠️ Override with reason
Viewer
   ❌ Approve
   ❌ Reject
   👁️ View only
However, for a simple/intermediate implementation, we can simplify this.
MVP:
Admin       → Full control
Manager     → Approve / Reject / Override
Accountant  → Review / Approve / Reject
Viewer      → Read only
This gives us a meaningful RBAC implementation for your mentor review.
6. Can an accountant manually override AI?
✅ Absolutely yes.
This is actually an important feature.
Suppose AI says:
ABC Technologies
Confidence: 92%
But the accountant knows:
"No, this payment came from XYZ Technologies."
The accountant can choose:
[ Override AI Match ]
Then:
AI Recommendation
       ↓
REJECT
       ↓
Manual Company Selection
       ↓
XYZ Technologies
       ↓
Accountant enters reason
       ↓
APPROVE
We should require a reason:
override_reason =
"Payment reference confirmed with bank statement."
And store it in audit_logs.
This is excellent for explaining human-in-the-loop AI to your mentor.
7. What happens when a payment is rejected?
There are two different types of rejection.
Case 1 — AI recommendation rejected
Example:
AI:
ABC Technologies → 94%
Accountant:
❌ Wrong match
We don't delete the payment.
Instead:
AI recommendation
       ↓
REJECTED
       ↓
Payment remains
       ↓
status = under_review
Then the accountant can manually investigate.
Case 2 — Actual payment rejected
Suppose the bank transaction is invalid, fraudulent, duplicated, or cannot be verified.
Then:
payments.status = rejected
The payment cannot be allocated.
The audit log records:
Who rejected?
Why?
When?
Example:
Reason:
Duplicate bank transaction detected.
8. How do we handle duplicate bank transactions?
This is another very important real-world problem.
Imagine the bank sends:
TXN12345
₹75,000
Then accidentally sends the same transaction again:
TXN12345
₹75,000
We don't want:
₹75,000
+
₹75,000
=
₹1,50,000
when the company actually paid only ₹75,000.
First protection
We already have:
transaction_reference VARCHAR(150) UNIQUE
So:
TXN12345
cannot normally be inserted twice.
9. But what if the bank doesn't provide a transaction reference?
Then we need a duplicate detection strategy.
We can compare:
sender
+
amount
+
payment date
+
bank reference
+
transaction reference
For example:
Payment A

ABC Technologies
Amount:
₹75,000
Date:
2026-08-05
Bank reference:
HDFC123
If another payment arrives with the same details, our system can flag:
⚠️ Possible duplicate payment
Then:
Payment
   ↓
Duplicate Detection
   ↓
Possible duplicate?
   ↓
YES
   ↓
Reconciliation Case
   ↓
Accountant Review
Important:
AI can detect and recommend, but the accountant should make the final decision.

12. One principle I want you to remember
This is the most important architecture concept for the whole project:
AI recommends. Humans approve. The database records the final financial decision.
So our system is not:
AI → Database
It is:
AI
 ↓
Recommendation
 ↓
Human Review
 ↓
Approval
 ↓
Database
 ↓
Audit Log
That gives us a much safer and more realistic Agentic AI financial application.
